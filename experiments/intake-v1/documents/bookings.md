# Tide Workshop bookings and cancellations

Synthetic demonstration policy. Effective 1 October 2026.

## Equipment bookings
Members book equipment through the member portal. The laser cutter has a maximum session length of 90 minutes. The sewing station has a maximum session length of 120 minutes. A member may hold at most two future equipment bookings at a time. Bookings may be made up to 14 days in advance. Required induction and a current endorsement must be completed before making a laser cutter booking.

## Arrival
Members must check in with the duty steward before a booked session. Arriving more than 15 minutes late releases the slot to the waiting list. A released late-arrival slot is treated as a late cancellation. The booking ends at its original end time even if a member arrives late.

## Cancellation and refunds
Cancel at least 24 hours before the session start to receive a full refund of the equipment booking fee. Cancellations less than 24 hours before the session start receive no refund. Exactly 24 hours before the session qualifies for a full refund. If the workshop cancels a session because equipment is unavailable, it refunds the full equipment booking fee regardless of notice. Refunds return to the original payment method within five working days. Membership fees and purchased materials are not refunded under this booking policy.

## Changes
A booking cannot be transferred to another member. To change equipment, cancel the existing booking and create a new one. The cancellation timing rules still apply. The assistant cannot create, change, cancel, or refund bookings; it can explain the documented process only.
