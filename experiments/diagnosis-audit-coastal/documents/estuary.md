# Estuary Instruments supplier conditions

Synthetic supplier for a demonstration. Prices and conditions are fictional. Effective 1 October 2026.

## Quotations and dispatch
Catalogue prices are in INR and exclude tax and shipping. The documents do not specify tax rates or shipping charges. Item subtotals therefore cannot be presented as final payable totals. Quoted lead times are working days from written order acceptance, not calendar days from a user query. The supplier operates Monday to Friday, excluding supplier holidays. The holiday calendar is not provided. Lead times are estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates. Live stock is not provided in the catalogue.

## Order process
The minimum order subtotal for Estuary Instruments is INR 3000 before tax and shipping. Orders must include an SKU, quantity, delivery address and purchase-order reference. The assistant can prepare an informational item subtotal but cannot place an order, reserve inventory or issue a purchase order. A mixed-supplier request is handled as separate orders; one supplier's subtotal cannot satisfy another supplier's minimum.

## Warranty
Estuary probes and loggers carry a 12-month manufacturing-defect warranty from dispatch. Batteries carry a six-month manufacturing-defect warranty. Cables carry a three-month manufacturing-defect warranty. Warranty excludes corrosion caused by damaged seals, accidental damage and unauthorized modifications. A claim needs the serial number and purchase-order reference. Contact support@estuary.example for a return authorization before sending equipment back.

## Compatibility
Use only the compatibility entries explicitly listed in the catalogue. A shared connector shape does not establish electrical or firmware compatibility. The supplier does not authorize adapters or substitutions in these documents.
