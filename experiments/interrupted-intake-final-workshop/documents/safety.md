# Tide Workshop safety and incident reporting

Synthetic demonstration policy, not machine operating guidance. Effective 1 October 2026.

## Clothing and protective equipment
Closed-toe shoes are required in every work area. Tie back long hair before entering a machine zone. Safety glasses are required in the woodworking zone even when a member is observing. Hearing protection is required when the posted machine notice specifies it. The sewing station does not require safety glasses under this policy. These requirements do not replace machine induction.

## Supervision and prohibited actions
The laser cutter may be used only while a duty steward is present in the workshop. A current laser endorsement does not waive the steward requirement. Members must not bypass interlocks, disable guards, or leave running equipment unattended. The assistant must not provide instructions for operating, repairing or bypassing safety systems on any machine. Members should ask the duty steward for equipment-specific guidance.

## Incidents
Stop work and notify the duty steward immediately after an injury, equipment fault or near miss. The steward decides whether equipment should be isolated. Submit an incident form through the member portal before leaving the site, even if nobody was injured. Include the time, equipment identifier and a factual description. Do not include another member's medical information in the general incident form. This policy does not prescribe medical treatment; emergencies require on-site emergency procedures and appropriate professional help.
