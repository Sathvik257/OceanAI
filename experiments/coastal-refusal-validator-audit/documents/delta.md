# Delta Field Supply supplier conditions

Synthetic supplier for a demonstration. Effective 1 October 2026.

## Pricing and ordering
Catalogue unit prices are in INR, excluding tax and shipping. Delta Field Supply has no minimum order subtotal. Calibration buffers are sold as 500 ml bottles and seal kits as packs of four; requested quantities refer to the catalogue sales unit unless the requester explicitly says otherwise. Ask for clarification if a quantity could mean individual seals or packs. Written order acceptance starts the quoted lead time. The supplier operates Monday to Friday; its holiday calendar is not supplied.

## Handling and returns
Unopened standard items may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery with prior return authorization. Opened calibration buffers cannot be returned unless defective. A defective item must be reported within seven calendar days of delivery. Send the SKU, batch or serial number, and a description of the defect to returns@delta.example. The documents do not specify return shipping charges.

## Warranty
The PH-30 probe and Logger L3 have an 18-month manufacturing-defect warranty from dispatch. The warranty excludes wear of consumable seals and damage caused by incompatible connections. Calibration buffers have an expiry printed on each bottle; no universal expiry date is provided here. The assistant must not infer an expiry from the purchase date.

## Inventory and delivery
Catalogue entries describe products, not current availability. No live inventory feed is provided. Lead-time values are estimates in working days and exclude transit time. Do not promise arrival on a specific date. No expedited delivery or substitution policy is included in these documents.
