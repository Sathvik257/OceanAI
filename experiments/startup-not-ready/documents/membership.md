# Tide Workshop membership and access

Synthetic demonstration policy. This is not a real workshop or safety standard.
Effective 1 October 2026. Applies to adult members at the Riverside site.

## Access
The Riverside workshop opens Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 20:00. It opens Saturday from 10:00 to 16:00. The workshop is closed on Sunday. Members must scan their own membership card when entering and leaving. Lending a membership card to another person is not permitted. The minimum membership age is 18 years.

## Induction
Every member must complete the general induction before entering a work area. General induction lasts 45 minutes and is booked through the member portal. Completing general induction does not authorize use of the laser cutter. Laser cutter access requires a separate laser induction and a current equipment endorsement. An endorsement expires 12 months after the induction date. A member with an expired endorsement must attend a refresher before booking that equipment.

## Membership and support
Standard membership costs INR 900 per month. Membership fees do not include equipment booking fees or materials. Members can pause membership once per calendar year for up to two months by emailing the membership desk before the next billing date. Pauses cannot be backdated. Policy questions and accessibility requests should go to membership@tide.example. Staff may offer help locating the applicable written policy; the assistant cannot approve exceptions.

## Missing policy
This document does not define guest admission, weekend guest access, or companion access. Members must contact the membership desk for those questions. Opening hours alone do not establish whether guests are allowed.
