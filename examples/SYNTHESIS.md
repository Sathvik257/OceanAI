# Synthetic corpus provenance

These fictional documents were drafted with Codex assistance for this repository. No actual company policy, safety certification, price, supplier relationship or purchasing result is claimed. `.example` email addresses are placeholders reserved for examples. The source prose and CSV are the complete synthetic inputs, not model-run evidence.

## Workshop synthesis prompt

Create three short, mutually consistent policy documents for a fictional adult community makerspace called Tide Workshop: membership/access, equipment bookings/refunds, and safety/incident reporting. Include precise numbers, exceptions and rules spanning documents (laser induction plus steward presence). Deliberately leave weekend guest access unsupported. Do not include operational machine instructions or medical treatment. Use Markdown and clearly label every document synthetic. Give enough distinct facts for at least 20 generated evaluation cases. Do not write the test questions.

## Coastal station synthesis prompt

Create a fictional coastal sensor procurement corpus: a CSV with twelve SKUs, explicit INR unit prices, sales units, supplier names, estimated working-day lead times and restricted compatibility; and two supplier policy documents with different minimum orders, warranty periods and return conditions. Include ambiguities around packs versus individual seals. Deliberately omit live stock, tax rates, shipping charges and guaranteed arrival dates. Clearly label the corpus synthetic and use reserved .example contacts. Do not write the evaluation questions.

## Review constraints

The CSV is synthetic too, even though its rows contain no prose disclaimer. Source values are fixed before any model evaluation. The factory must generate questions from these sources, preserve source references, and report errors instead of changing the corpus to inflate scores.
